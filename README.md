# Simtha's Smart Suite

Simtha's Smart Buyers – AI Business Productivity Assistant (Lovable AI Prompt)

Build a professional, modern AI-powered web application called Simtha's Smart Buyers – AI Business Productivity Assistant.

Business Description: Simtha's Smart Buyers is a South African business that sells affordable hair pieces, wigs, weaves, braids, closures, frontals, hair extensions, cellphones, and phone accessories. The application should help the business owner communicate with customers, manage daily business tasks, summarize meetings, and provide AI-powered customer support.

The application must be responsive on mobile and desktop and have a premium Black and Gold design that reflects elegance, affordability, and technology.
